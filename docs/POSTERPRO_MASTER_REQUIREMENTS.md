## Requirement acceptance rules

- Live marketplace mutations always require an operator-approved item-level preview.
- Calculated shipping requires measured parcel facts, origin, and provider eligibility; estimates cannot satisfy that condition.
- A local job completion is never remote publication confirmation.
- A draft enters Needs Review only after identity, category, price evidence, images, shipping, and marketplace validation are satisfied or an explicit operator exception is recorded.
